Note: Holidays marked * (Hari Raya Puasa, Hari Raya Qurban, Awal Ramadan and Deepavali) are subject to change. Holidays that fall on a rest day may be replaced by a holiday on the next working day; replacement holidays are announced separately and are not shown here. Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu observe a Friday–Saturday weekend; all other states and federal territories observe Saturday–Sunday. Federal public holidays apply nationwide, except: the second day of Chinese New Year is not a federal holiday in Kelantan and Terengganu, the second day of Hari Raya Qurban is a federal holiday only in Kelantan and Terengganu, and Deepavali is not a public holiday in Sarawak.
