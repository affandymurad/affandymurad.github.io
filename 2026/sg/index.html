Stretches of three or more consecutive days off (weekends and public holidays).

Note: Vesak Day (Sunday 31 May), National Day (Sunday 9 August) and Deepavali (Sunday 8 November) fall on Sundays, so the following Mondays (1 June, 10 August and 9 November) are replacement public holidays. Hari Raya Puasa (21 March) falls on a Saturday. Employees covered by the Employment Act are entitled to 11 paid public holidays. Those required to work on a public holiday are entitled to an extra day's salary at the basic rate of pay, in addition to their gross rate of pay for that day. Employers and employees may mutually agree to substitute a public holiday for another working day.
